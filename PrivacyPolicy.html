概述

「玉富Hub」（Yufu Hub，以下简称「本应用」）是一款离线休闲游戏合集。我们重视您的隐私。本政策说明本应用如何处理信息。

我们收集的信息

本应用不要求注册账号，也不主动收集姓名、邮箱、电话号码等可直接识别您身份的信息。

为提供游戏功能，以下数据仅保存在您的设备本地（例如通过系统提供的本地存储机制）：

  • 各小游戏的进度、分数与最佳纪录（如晨间评分、投掷距离、准点率、矿石数量等）；
  • 应用内设置（如震动、音效开关）；
  • 教程完成状态及类似的本地偏好。

上述数据不会由本应用自动上传至我们的服务器（本应用以离线使用为主，且不依赖账户体系）。

我们如何使用信息

  • 在 Hub 与小游戏中展示您的纪录与进度；
  • 记住您的设置与偏好；
  • 在设备上提供「清除工区账本」等本地数据管理功能（若您主动使用）。

第三方与跟踪

就本应用当前版本而言：

  • 不包含第三方广告 SDK；
  • 不向广告或数据分析公司提供您的个人数据用于跨应用跟踪；
  • 不使用需要联网账号的社交登录。

若未来版本引入可选的联网功能或第三方服务，我们将更新本政策并在应用内或 App Store 页面说明。

儿童隐私

本应用面向一般休闲游戏用户。我们不会 knowingly 向 13 周岁以下儿童索取个人信息。若您认为儿童在未经监护人同意的情况下向我们提供了信息，请通过下方联系方式与我们联系。

数据安全与保留

本地数据受您设备的安全机制（如设备密码、加密）保护。卸载本应用通常将删除其本地存储的游戏数据。我们建议您自行备份设备。

您的权利与选择

  • 在应用「Shed / 设置」中关闭震动或音效；
  • 使用应用内提供的清除进度功能删除本地纪录；
  • 通过卸载应用删除本地数据。

政策变更

我们可能不时更新本政策。更新后的版本将在此页面发布并修改生效日期。重大变更时，我们可能通过 App Store 更新说明或应用内提示告知您。

English

Overview

Yufu Hub (“the App”) is an offline casual game hub. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

Information We Collect

The App does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or other directly identifying information.

To run gameplay features, the following is stored only on your device (e.g., local storage APIs):

  • Game progress, scores, and personal bests (dawn marks, cast distance, on-schedule rates, ore counts, etc.);
  • In-app settings (such as haptics and sounds);
  • Tutorial completion flags and similar local preferences.

This data is not automatically uploaded to our servers by the App in its current offline, account-free design.

How We Use Information

  • Display your records and progress in the Hub and mini games;
  • Remember your settings;
  • Support local actions such as clearing saved progress when you choose to do so in Settings.

Third Parties and Tracking

For the current version of the App:

  • No third-party advertising SDKs are included;
  • We do not share your personal data with ad or analytics companies for cross-app tracking;
  • No social sign-in that requires an online account.

If a future version adds optional online features or third-party services, we will update this policy and disclose them on the App Store listing and/or in the App.

Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for general casual audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information without parental consent, please contact us using the details below.

Security and Retention

Local data is protected by your device’s security features. Uninstalling the App typically removes its local game data. You are responsible for backing up your device if needed.

Your Choices

  • Turn off haptics or sounds in Shed (Settings);
  • Clear local progress using in-app controls;
  • Delete local data by uninstalling the App.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes may also be described in App Store release notes or in-app notices.
